# Ideal State — Business Dashboard

Owner: Gui. Altitude: product capability. Form: lean. Status: draft, unregistered.
Source of truth: this document. Horizon: first useful founder review.
Refresh: on source, privacy, metric-definition or user-feedback changes.

## Purpose

A founder can review the selected businesses on a phone, identify the next
important decision, and trace every displayed observation to dated evidence.

## Desired state

Business outcomes, delivery activity and data quality are separate. The interface
reduces context switching without creating a new authority for source records.
Unknowns remain visible. Business grouping prevents repository double counting.

## Anti-state

No invented metrics, progress percentages, acceptance, causal lift or time saved.
No private information in public documentation, search, bundles or build output.
No silent revival of parked work. No financial or customer record mutations.

## Claims and evidence

- BD-01: unauthorized readers cannot obtain business payloads. Verify session,
  API, RSC and public-asset negative paths before private activation.
- BD-02: every observation retains source and time. Test projection and freshness.
- BD-03: mobile review reveals next actions and evidence. Browser review and
  subsequent owner feedback; usefulness is not established by passing tests.
- BD-04: incomplete coverage never implies portfolio completeness. Visible
  coverage and unconfigured states; no outcome or ISA promotion by agents.
